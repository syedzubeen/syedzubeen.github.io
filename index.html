Small, offline-first apps.

A short showcase of the apps I build. Each one is a small, focused Android tool designed to do a single job well: offline-first, with no account required and no subscription. Some are free to use and supported by ads; each app's privacy policy states exactly what that means for it.

How these are built

One idea behind every app on this page.

  1. 01

    Works offline

    The core of each app runs without a connection. Cloud is opt-in, never a requirement to get the job done.

  2. 02

    No accounts, no subscriptions

    Open the app and use it. No sign-up walls and no recurring charges, just a single tool that does one job cleanly.

  3. 03

    Honest about ads

    Some apps are free and ad-supported. Each app's privacy policy says plainly what that means and what it shares.
