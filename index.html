How these are built

One idea behind every app on this page.

  1. 01

    Private by default

    Your data stays on your device. Nothing is collected, and nothing is sold — the apps have no reason to phone home.

  2. 02

    Works offline

    The core of each app runs without a connection. Cloud is opt-in, never a requirement to get the job done.

  3. 03

    No accounts, no clutter

    Open the app and use it. No sign-up walls, no ads, no upsells — a single tool that does one job cleanly.
